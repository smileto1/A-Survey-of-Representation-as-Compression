# 🔵 Functional Representations (Sec. 3)

Functional methods fit a query-conditioned observation operator and reconstruct by evaluating, interpolating or integrating a function- or field-like state (weights, modulations, latents, grids, tensors, hash features). The fitted state need not be the whole compressed object: a payload may combine weights, latents, grids, codebooks, masks and side information; the survey reserves the term codec for methods that specify a decodable serialization, all decoder-required state and complete payload accounting.
